Who we are

Design lab focused on practical, good-looking parts.

We sit between design and fabrication: refining concepts, engineering them for print, and finishing parts so they feel intentional. Our workflow spans digital sculpting, parametric tools, and hands-on finishing in resin, ABS, and silicone.

Brand & products

Logos that work as physical objects and desktop-ready products that ship as STL, resin, or painted pieces.

Automation mindset

We build configurators that turn hours of manual modeling into seconds, keeping production consistent.

Repair & retrofit

Reverse engineering to keep legacy gear running—replacement brackets, printer jigs, and custom mounts.
